## When to Replace Your Roof

A full replacement becomes the right call when you see signs like these:

- **Roof age:** the roof is 15–25 years old
- **Storm damage** across a wide area of the roof
- **Leaks** that keep coming back
- **Missing or curling shingles**, or worn tiles
- **Sagging**, or daylight visible in the attic

An inspection confirms the condition of the roof before you decide between repair and replacement.

## Roofing Materials Compared

- **Shingle:** affordable, with a shorter service life.
- **Tile:** durable and the classic Sarasota look, but heavier — the structure has to support the weight.
- **Metal:** the longest service life, with the top wind and storm resistance.

## Permits and Florida Building Code Wind Requirements

A permit is required for roof replacement in Sarasota, FL. The work must meet Florida Building Code wind requirements, and the project goes through staged inspections before it is signed off.
